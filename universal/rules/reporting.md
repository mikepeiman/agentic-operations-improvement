# Reporting

Lead with the outcome.

Report:

1. completed work;
2. observed verification;
3. unverified acceptance boundaries;
4. remaining work and genuine blockers;
5. authoritative tracker state;
6. delivered commit, build, run, or output artifact.

Distinguish observed fact, source evidence, inference, estimate,
recommendation, and open question. Do not label work complete when the claimed
boundary was not exercised.

Use one stable term for one concept. Describe product decisions through
concrete actions and results. Use `MUST` and `MUST NOT` only for observable
requirements. Do not use rhetorical padding, invented opposition,
grandiosity, or self-certifying labels in place of evidence.

Explain non-obvious reasons in durable comments, decisions, or commit bodies
where future maintainers need them. The completion report should let a reader
locate the work and understand its limits without reconstructing chat history.

**Compliance:** the report identifies what changed, what was observed, what
remains uncertain, and where the durable records live.
