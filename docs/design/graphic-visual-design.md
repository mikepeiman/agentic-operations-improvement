# Graphic / Visual Design Guidance

Use for posters, editorial layouts, title cards, social graphics, visual campaigns, presentation art direction, packaging concepts, typography-led compositions, and general graphic design.

## Primary references

- **It's Nice That — Graphic Design** — https://www.itsnicethat.com/graphic-design  
  Broad, high-quality contemporary graphic design and art direction.
- **Fonts In Use** — https://fontsinuse.com/  
  Exceptional archive for seeing typography applied in real identities, posters, books, packaging, software, and websites.
- **BP&O** — https://bpando.org/

- **Behance — Graphic Design** — https://www.behance.net/search/projects/graphic%20design

- **The Design Blog** — https://thedsgnblog.com/  
  Useful gateway to contemporary graphic design, typography, identity, print, and specialist inspiration sources.

## Guidance

Begin with communication intent and visual hierarchy, not decoration.

Reference work from the appropriate medium: poster references for posters, editorial for editorial, packaging for packaging. Each has different constraints and reading behavior.

Study:
- typography,
- composition,
- scale,
- rhythm,
- contrast,
- grid,
- image treatment,
- negative space,
- color relationships,
- repetition and visual systems.

Typography is a primary design material, not text placed after the visual design is finished.

Seek a clear visual idea. Avoid compensating for a weak concept with gradients, glow, random shapes, excessive effects, or arbitrary ornament.

Use references to widen the solution space, not to collapse everything into the current dominant trend.

Make several directions meaningfully different in composition and visual language before polishing one.

**Working principle:** communicate one strong visual idea with deliberate typography, composition, and hierarchy; remove elements that do not strengthen it.
