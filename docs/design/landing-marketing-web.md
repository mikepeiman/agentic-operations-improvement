## Guidance

Start from the page's job: communicate an offer, establish trust, create desire, and lead toward a clear next action.

Research pages with a similar audience, product type, price/commitment level, and conversion goal—not merely the same visual trend.

Study:
- hero composition,
- value proposition hierarchy,
- proof and credibility,
- product demonstration,
- section rhythm,
- CTA placement,
- typography and art direction,
- responsive/mobile behavior.

Do not assemble a generic sequence of oversized hero + three cards + testimonials + CTA simply because it is common.

Use strong visual identity, but keep the proposition understandable within seconds.

Marketing design may be more expressive than product UI, but interaction should remain legible. Motion and novelty should strengthen hierarchy and product story rather than obstruct them.

Use real product imagery/interface where it communicates value better than abstract decoration.

**Working principle:** understand the conversion story first, then use excellent web references to make that story unusually clear and visually memorable.
